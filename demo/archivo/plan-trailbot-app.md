# First trailbot plan: a mobile app (2025)

The first plan was a mobile app with offline maps, GPS tracking and a social feed of hikes.

Why it was dropped:
- Two app stores, alone, meant months before the first user.
- GPS tracking meant storing where people are, which Alex did not want to do.
- The people who would use it already plan hikes in Telegram groups.

It became a Telegram bot in May 2025 (ADR 0001). Offline maps are still in the ideas backlog.
